Challenger Route & ALPR Map

An interactive route map combining the travel endpoints described in the public case record with the current OpenStreetMap/DeFlock ALPR camera layer for Utah. Route lines are reconstructed on current roads from the disclosed endpoints; the public warrant excerpts reviewed for this project do not publish a turn-by-turn GPS breadcrumb trail for the full St. George–Orem drive.

Evidence distinction: red, orange, and blue routes represent warrant/court-described endpoints reconstructed over current roads. The purple route connects separately reported locations from the livestream, including Panguitch and the Cedar City Maverik. Camera markers are current community-mapped ALPR locations, not proof that the same camera was installed or operating on September 10–11, 2025.

What each line represents

Morning St. George → UVU

The court record says Robinson left the St. George residence in the early morning and traveled to Utah Valley University, where a gray Challenger attributed to him entered the campus garage around 8:29–8:30 a.m. The public text does not specify every road or turn.

12:46 Mountain Shadows → Quick Quack

A later warrant affidavit says a stored Google Maps route began near Mountain Shadows Shopping Center around 12:46 p.m. and ran to Quick Quack Car Wash at 855 N State St in Orem; investigators said the Challenger arrived around 12:57.

1:33 UVU-area → St. George

The same affidavit describes a second stored Google Maps route beginning around 1:33 a.m. September 11 from a neighborhood north of, and near, the rifle-recovery area by UVU, with the St. George residence as the destination.

Panguitch / Cedar City

The Panguitch restaurant stop is a separately reported and disputed location from the livestream rather than a warrant route endpoint. The Cedar City Maverik stop is supported by surveillance reporting around 7:15 a.m. September 11.
